### Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs)

| Technique                        | Description                                                                 | Example                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Port Hopping**                 | Allows adversaries to randomly change ports and protocols during a session. | —                                                                                              |
| **Using Non-Standard Ports**     | Running applications over ports not typically assigned to them.             | Running Yahoo! Messenger over TCP port 80 (HTTP) instead of the standard TCP port 5050.        |
| **Tunneling**                    | Tunneling within commonly used services to disguise traffic.                | Running peer-to-peer (P2P) file sharing or an IM client such as Meebo over HTTP.               |
| **Hiding Within SSL Encryption** | Masking application traffic using encryption.                               | Running traffic over TCP port 443 (HTTPS). More than half of all web traffic is now encrypted. |

#### Risk Associated with SaaS Applications
- Exposure of Confidential Information
- Cloud-Based Threats
- Unsanctioned SaaS Applicatons
- Lack of Visibility and Control
For **SaaS** applications,  just as in any other part of the network, the IT team is responsible for protecting and controlling the data, regardless of its location.

	Malicious Outsiders
	Malicious Insiders
	Accidental Data Exposure
	Accidental Share
	Promiscuous Share (Shared to someone who should not have access)
	Ghost (or Stale) Share (Shared of someone who is no longer active)

##### **Compliance and Security**

### Standards and Regulations

#### Payment Card Industry’s Data Security Standard
The Payment Card Industry's Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) establishes its own cybersecurity standards and best practices for businesses and organizations that allow payment card purchases

#### European Union General Data Protection Regulations
The European Union (EU) General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR) apply to any organization that does business with EU citizens.

### MITRE ATT&CK and Project FMX
The objective of FMX was to investigate how endpoint telemetry data and analytics could help improve post-intrusion detection of attackers operating within enterprise networks. The ATT&CK framework was used as the basis for testing the efficacy of the sensors and analytics under FMX and served as the common language both offense and defense could use to improve over time
- ATT&CK for Enterprise
- ATT&CK for Mobiles
- Pre-ATT&CK

### CVE - Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures
The goal of the system is to make it easier to share vulnerability data across stakeholders, including software vendors, tool vendors, security practitioners, and end users.


- Its possible to view CVEs from **Palo Alto Cortex XDR**

### CVSS - Common Vulnerability Scoring System
The Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS) offers a method for enumerating a vulnerability's key characteristics and generating a numerical score that reflects the vulnerability's severity.
