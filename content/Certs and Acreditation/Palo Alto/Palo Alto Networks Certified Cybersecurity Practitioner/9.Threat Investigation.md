
## What Is a Security Operations Investigation?

A security operations investigation is a process conducted by a specialized team responsible for monitoring for cybersecurity threats and responding to incidents.

The goal of a security operations investigation is to identify, analyze, and mitigate security incidents to protect digital assets and data and to improve the organization's security posture.
