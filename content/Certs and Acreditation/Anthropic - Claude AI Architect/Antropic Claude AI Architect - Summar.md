## Announcement of Cert Tracker
I'm studying for the Claude Certified Architect – Foundations exam.  
  
The exam guide is a PDF. The prep material I found (and like) is spread across three different sites that overlap by ~20 hours of video. My study plan was a text file that went stale in a week.  
  
So I built the thing I wanted instead: [**https://lnkd.in/ghDgFwJY**](https://www.linkedin.com/safety/go/?url=https%3A%2F%2Flnkd%2Ein%2FghDgFwJY&urlhash=CVl6&mt=YZ8YgF0w-8MdVQKfgH904SLsGn-f2RR3hx5RNgWupMHmMDbwc6f1idNRwN4Ll-0veFeJznk7H8c6yvJ1Mo62YGZvA0FsAk6cC_uFwQa5gqKQ9C85JCOsefZaCg&isSdui=true)  
  
It covers all four Anthropic certifications — Associate, Developer, Architect Foundations, and Architect Professional. For each one:  
  
• A day-by-day plan built from the official exam guide, with every task tagged to the guide's own task statement — so you can verify coverage instead of hoping you didn't miss a domain.  
• Every task links to the exact doc page, course module, or guide section it needs. Every URL was checked live, because docs sites reorganize constantly.  
• Pick your training source: Anthropic's free courses only, + freeCodeCamp's free 12-hour course, or + the paid Udemy one. The plan filters to just that track. No more scheduling three overlapping video courses for me!  
• Log your official result and it shows your renew-by date. These certs are only valid 12 months.  
• Notes per task, per-domain confidence tracking, and progress that reflects the track you actually chose.  
  
It's free, there's nothing to install, and signup is open. I built it for myself, but it's multi-user, so help yourself if it's useful.  
  
Built it with Claude Code, which felt like the right way to study for a Claude exam
