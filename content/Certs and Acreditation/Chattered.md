## Cyber Security Council Purpose
To give the individual a quick way to demonstrate skills and competence within the Cyber Security area across the UK, under the national framework requirement demonstrate skills as recognize Cyber Security Professional.
The objective of the Cyber Security Council on its own words are:

“*To promote high standards of practice in the cyber security profession for the benefit of the public. In particular, but not exclusively, by advancing education in the subject of cyber security and through the development, promotion and stewardship of nationally recognised standards for the cyber security profession.*” For in depth detail read the following [Cyber Security Counci.pdfl](https://www.ukcybersecuritycouncil.org.uk/base/media/docs/v-4-21-12-22-uk-csc-spcc.pdf)

## Objective
To earn and become Chartered Cyber Security Professional (ChCSP)

# Cyber Security Levels
- Chartered - ChCSP – Chartered Cyber Security Professional
- Principal - PCSP – Principal Cyber Security Professional
- Associate - ACSP – Associate Cyber Security Professional

 Achieved the required level of experience and demonstrated the appropriate breadth and depth of competence and commitment for their category of registration, including specialism; • Proved their ability and commitment to maintaining and improving their skills; and • Made a commitment to adhere to codes of conduct, practice, and ethics.

### CIISEC Roadmap
One of the Licensing Bodies that let you gain the qualification is the [CIISEC](https://www.ciisec.org/chartering/)
Members of CIISec can apply to obtain professional registration with the UK Cyber Security Council in the following specialisms: [Cyber Security Governance & Risk Management](https://www.ukcybersecuritycouncil.org.uk/cms/media/zpdbrdab/contextualisation-governance-risk-v3.pdf), [Secure System Architecture & Design](https://www.ukcybersecuritycouncil.org.uk/cms/media/u5ukmjyv/contextualisation-secure-system-architecture-design-v2.pdf), and [Cyber Security Audit and Assurance](https://www.ukcybersecuritycouncil.org.uk/cms/media/cnsnafh2/contextualisation-audit-and-assurance-v4.pdf).

#### **CIISEC Process & Assesment**
All the steps below are found here https://www.ciisec.org/chartering/
1. Become a member of CIISEC, register as a member (£80 p/year)
2. Revise the Council’s [SPCC](https://ukcscproductionv2.blob.core.windows.net/media/docs/standard-for-professional-competence-and-commitment-uk-csc-spcc-4-2.pdf) for the skills

4. Pay the fees (£840 assessment for Char/Principal ~ $600 for Assoc level)
5. Complete the assessment

#### **Cyber Scheme Process & Assessment**
![[Pasted image 20260909124317.png]]

**Requirements Security Testing specialism only**:
- For **Chartered Title**, applicants are required to pass or hold a qualifying certificate – The Cyber Scheme’s CSTL exam (App or Inf) or Cyber Scheme Red Team Manager (CSRTM) exam, _and_ be able to demonstrate significant delivery experience at Team Leader level to proceed with their application. Note: We recognise equivalent exams from other organisations where they are recognised by the Council.

- For **Principal Title** applications, registrants are required to pass or hold The Cyber Scheme’s CSTL exam (App or Inf), or equivalent exams from other organisations. 

- For **Practitioner Title** applications, registrants are required to pass or hold The Cyber Scheme’s CSTM exam, or equivalent exams from other organisations. 

- The **Associate Title** is not specialism-specific but can be the first step on the ladder to Practitioner in Security Testing. Please click [here](https://thecyberscheme.org/associate-title/) for further details.
