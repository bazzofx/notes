Microsoft Defender for Identity is a cloud-native security solution integrated with Microsoft Defender XDR. It feeds on-premises AD and Microsoft Entra ID signals into correlated alerts and incidents within the unified Defender portal. This enables joint visibility across cloud and on-premises identity activities—alerts from Defender for Identity, Cloud Apps, and Endpoint are automatically correlated into incidents without extra integration.


Microsoft Defender for Identity complements Defender for Endpoint in the unified platform:

- **Defender for Identity** monitors domain controller traffic.
- **Defender for Endpoint** monitors endpoints.  
    Both contribute signals to the shared incident and alert queue in the Defender portal. You can also view Defender for Identity alerts tied to a specific endpoint directly from that device's page.

## Other integrations

- **Privileged Access Management (PAM)** platforms — CyberArk, Delinea, and BeyondTrust for threat detection on managed privileged accounts.
    
- **Okta** — monitors Okta sign-ins and activity to detect suspicious events in hybrid cloud-idP environments.
    
- **Microsoft Security Copilot** — feeds identity insights into Copilot for faster, natural-language–driven triage and investigation.
